Floor privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026.

Who uses Floor

Floor is a personal app with exactly one user: its author. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data is accessed

With my consent, Floor reads through the Google Health API, read-only:

  • daily step totals (googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly);
  • sleep sessions and sleep stages (googlehealth.sleep.readonly).

No other Google user data is requested, and Floor never writes to Google Health.

How it is used

The data is used only to calculate wake-up time, sleep duration and steps, and to send me a short daily summary in my own Telegram chat.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is stored only on my own computer.
  • It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the local database removes all stored data.
